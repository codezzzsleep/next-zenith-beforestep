the runnable source, real Demo, report, and a clear technical and asset iteration
timeline. Lux3D participates in the core process. Keep source, runtime assets,
curated evidence, and presentation materials organized by their purpose.

and `docs/decisions.md` before architectural or evidence-retention changes.

communicate verified project results; they are not the project's engineering
objective. Keep recorded playback, live simulation and hardware capabilities
accurately identified.

provenance, representative rejected assets, and real execution outcomes. A
truth-geometry diagnostic must never be presented as a Lux3D success.

`.git/agent-memory.md` when present. They identify the private authoritative
