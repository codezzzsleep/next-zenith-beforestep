OBSERVE · IMAGINE · REHEARSE · ACT

Rehearse the side
you cannot see.

What

A wheel-leg robot uses real RGB-D observations, Lux3D geometry candidates, observation checks and full physics rehearsals before acting in an independent simulation.

Why

Solar darkness removes reliable depth. Rocks also hide space from view. Changing viewpoint helps visibility but does not change sunlight; plausible hidden geometry can still be wrong.

How

Generate multiple real hypotheses, register them, reject inconsistencies with frozen observation rules, build occupancy, rehearse each admitted world with the same controller, then execute and review. Rejected hypotheses are tested through counterfactual interventions.

Results

The original narrow-corridor Lux run arrived. The preregistered suite also contains two real imagination collisions. The later strict method is a post-result revision; known-scene retests and designated holdouts are reported separately. Early v2 collided in F1_003. Final revision statistics are being integrated from the completed runs.
